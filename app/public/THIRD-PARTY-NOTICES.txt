Visual assets

Oak floor maps: Poly Haven CC0 Laminate Floor 02, https://polyhaven.com/a/laminate_floor_02 . Images are resized WebP derivatives; DirectX normals are converted to OpenGL on load. Other surfaces use authored procedural maps or material values.

Duvet: authored Blender cloth simulation reused as a generic furniture asset, resized for this model. Pillows and other furniture are authored geometry.

Property photographs and the main plan are user-supplied source material. They are distinct from the generated 3D reconstruction and retain their original rights.

Refinement 2 assets (Poly Haven, CC0):
https://polyhaven.com/a/modern_arm_chair_01 — scanned cushion components fitted to authored seating.
https://polyhaven.com/a/potted_plant_02 — countertop and table plants.
https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_leather — leather normal/roughness maps.
https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen — fabric normal/roughness maps.
https://polyhaven.com/a/white_oak_veneer — furniture colour/normal/roughness maps.
https://polyhaven.com/a/grass_ground — garden surface maps.
Maps are local 1K WebP derivatives from the existing project assets. Room reflection probes are generated from this property's modeled scene. Scanned assets are generic visual components, not scans of the house's exact furniture.
