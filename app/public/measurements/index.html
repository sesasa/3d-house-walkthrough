These are image-derived dimensions, not a survey. The ranges below are conservative working estimates of uncertainty, not statistical confidence intervals. Photo perspectives and this indicative plan cannot establish millimetre accuracy.

The entrance step

The earlier model applied the entrance's 1.87 m label to its whole width. The supplied plan shows a step beside the guest WC. Applying 1.87 m to the wider end allows the main kitchen, office and northern room widths to close without changing the labelled bedrooms.

7.57 kitchen + 0.12 partition + 3.47 office = 11.16 m.
North: 2.40 utility + 0.12 partition + 2.28 bathroom + 0.12 partition + 1.66 upper hall + 0.12 partition + 4.46 bedroom = 11.16 m.

The difference 1.87 − 1.66 = 0.21 m is the inferred step depth. The existence of the step is plan-backed; its exact depth and the assumed 0.12 m walls require on-site confirmation. The guest WC's lower footprint follows this stepped boundary.

Opening measurements

Each opening is scaled against its own clear wall: opening pixel span ÷ wall pixel span × room length. Local scaling avoids treating the entire drawing as uniformly scaled. Widths are rounded to centimetres; frame and reveal edges remain ambiguous by several pixels. Vertical heights still come from photo proportions.

Example: living west window = (786 − 722) ÷ (833 − 558) × 5.00 = 1.16 m, replacing the earlier 1.81 m estimate. The photo confirms a single window beside the garden door, with a horizontal division. The front-door calculation uses the inferred 1.66 m upper-hall width rather than a printed dimension.

What still needs measurement

Door hinge positions, exact cabinet module widths/depths, window heights and sill levels, external wall thickness and pitched ceiling heights remain estimates. The living-room H:267 annotation is retained as 2.67 m at the modeled ridge, but its intended datum is unclear. Cabinet forms and positions were checked against multiple photographs; the material appearance is illustrative.

The first useful checks are the two entrance widths, the WC return depth, one ceiling/eave height, and the kitchen counter lengths. These would constrain the remaining uncertainty more effectively than further visual guesses.
