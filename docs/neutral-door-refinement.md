Compass directions use plan north; X is east and Z south. Leaves are shown at
90 degrees as a walkthrough presentation choice, not a measured stop angle.
The photos show different amounts of opening; IMG_9959 shows the double leaves
folded much farther back. Swing side and hinge jamb follow the main plan.
Door leaves are static and retain the existing navigation system's door-free
collision passages; they are not interactive physics doors.

The shared bathroom/hall wall now has white paint on the bathroom face rather
than hall yellow on both sides. Porcelain is neutral white with a softer glaze;
toilets have closed oval lids as photographed. Stone has quieter variation and
a muted neutral taupe. Neutral sky/sunlight, softer sunlight, and much weaker,
broader WC fill replace the previous strongly coloured lighting. Main bathroom
fill approximates the photographed ceiling illumination; its photometry is not
measured. Lightmaps and reflections are regenerated for the corrected geometry. An offline
lighting-only grade retains 35% of light colour variation and raises the
deepest shadows, especially in wet rooms. It leaves the material colours
separate; this is a presentation preference, not a photometric measurement.

Previous production source, baked assets, website and ZIP are preserved under

not a survey of dimensions or a claim of exact fixture manufacturer models.
