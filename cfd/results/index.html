Winter assumptions & model confidence +

This is a scenario prediction, not a measured temperature survey. It uses the retained calibrated house layout, an equivalent flat 2.50 m ceiling, open internal doorways and closed external doors/windows. Furniture, people, appliances, solar gain, infiltration and surface-to-surface radiation are omitted.

Supply: living room 70, bedroom 40 and office 40 m³/h. Extract: kitchen approximately 50, bryggers 35, bathroom 45 and WC 20 m³/h. The kitchen pressure outlet accommodates a small density-related balancing adjustment. Terminal positions and effective diffuser areas are assumed. The bedroom count follows the available plan: one main bedroom plus the office/bedroom.

Supply air is prescribed at 17.4°C: −5 + 0.90 × [21 − (−5)] − 1°C duct loss. Recovery is an assumed effective value, using a reference extract temperature of 21°C; the heat exchanger is not dynamically coupled to the house simulation. Defrost and bypass cycling are excluded.

Fixed assumed inside surface temperatures: heated floor 23.5°C, wet-room floor 25°C, ceiling 20.35°C, opaque external walls 19.86°C, glazing 15.8°C and external doors 17.1°C. Internal partitions are adiabatic. These describe an assumed heated state; they do not predict heater sizing or fabric performance. Section values are interpolated vertically from solved cell centres.

Numerical verification is documented in the project files.

The supplied shop link currently describes the ECO 400 XL. Genvex describes ECO 400 recovery up to 96%; the 90% value here is a scenario assumption, not a guarantee. The presentation takes visual inspiration from Aerotak’s temperature mapping case. No physical calibration has been performed.
